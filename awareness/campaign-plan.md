## Theme: "Digital Access & Cyber Safety in Rural Habitations"

**Academic Requirement**: CSP Week 2 Deliverable — Community Awareness Activity  
**Target Audience**: Village residents, farmers, self-help group (SHG) members, youth, and elders.

---

## 1. Campaign Objectives
1. **Promote Safe Digital Access**: Educate citizens on distinguishing authentic government welfare portals (`.gov.in` / `.nic.in`) from unofficial or deceptive websites.
2. **Prevent Cyber Fraud**: Raise awareness about common rural financial fraud vectors (spurious lottery links, fake DBT update calls, unsolicited OTP sharing).
3. **Introduce the Village Information Portal**: Demonstrate how to find local emergency numbers, hospital timings, school details, and verified welfare checklists on mobile phones.
4. **Foster Community Feedback**: Collect resident feedback regarding local information gaps to improve portal records.

## 3. Session Flow (120 Minutes)

| Time | Duration | Segment | Content & Activity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10:30 - 10:45 | 15 mins | **Introduction & Context** | Welcome remarks; explaining the purpose of the Community Service Project; brief findings from Week 1 household survey. |
| 10:45 - 11:15 | 30 mins | **Module 1: Identifying Official Government Portals** | Explaining internet domain structures (`.gov.in` and `.nic.in` vs `.com` or `.org`); avoiding cyber-cafe overcharging; verifying document checklists before visiting mandal offices. |
| 11:15 - 11:45 | 30 mins | **Module 2: Rural Cyber Safety & Fraud Prevention** | Golden rules of mobile safety: Never share OTP or UPI PIN with callers claiming to be bank or government agents; identifying fraudulent SMS/WhatsApp links; how to report scams via National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930). |
| 11:45 - 12:15 | 30 mins | **Module 3: Village Information Portal Walkthrough** | Live demonstration of the portal on mobile screens: Emergency one-touch dialing, checking PHC doctor timings, exploring local artisan listings, and submitting corrections. |
| 12:15 - 12:30 | 15 mins | **Q&A, Feedback & Conclusion** | Open question-and-answer round; distributing printed handouts; collecting participant feedback forms and signatures on the attendance sheet. |
