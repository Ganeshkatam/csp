### Part 1: How to Spot Genuine Government Websites

Many unofficial websites mimic government schemes and charge illegal fees for services that are completely free. Remember these key rules:

1. **Look at the End of the Web Address**:
   - Official Central and State Government portals always end in **`.gov.in`** or **`.nic.in`** (e.g., `pmkisan.gov.in`, `pmjay.gov.in`, `meebhoomi.ap.gov.in`).
   - If a website ends in `.com`, `.org`, `.xyz`, or `.net`, it is **NOT an official government website**, even if it displays the national emblem.
2. **Never Pay for Scheme Application Guidelines**:
   - Guidelines, eligibility criteria, and required document checklists are publicly available for free on government portals.
3. **Check for Security Padlock**:
   - Ensure the website URL begins with `https://` and displays a security lock icon in the browser address bar.

### Part 2: Rural Cyber Safety — 4 Golden Rules

1. **Never Share OTP (One-Time Password)**:
   - No bank officer, government official, or scheme coordinator will EVER ask for your OTP over a telephone call.
   - An OTP is like the key to your bank locker. If you share it, money can be stolen instantly from your account.
2. **Never Share Bank PIN or UPI PIN**:
   - Remember: Entering a UPI PIN is only required to **SEND money**, never to receive money or government subsidies!
3. **Beware of Fake WhatsApp / SMS Links**:
   - Ignore messages stating: *"Click here to get Rs. 50,000 under Government Subsidy Scheme."* These are phishing traps designed to steal personal data.
4. **Where to Report Fraud Immediately**:
   - **National Cyber Crime Helpline**: Dial **1930**
   - **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal**: `https://cybercrime.gov.in`

### Part 3: Using the [Assigned Village Name] Information Portal

Our village now has a dedicated digital information portal designed specifically for mobile phones:

- **Emergency Contacts**: One-touch call buttons for PHC ambulance, Police, Panchayat Secretary, and Electricity Lineman.
- **Healthcare Timings**: Check Primary Health Centre doctor OPD hours and immunization schedules before leaving home.
- **Welfare Schemes**: Verified lists of required documents for farmer, health, and pension schemes so you avoid repeated mandal office trips.
- **Local Services**: Phone numbers of village tailors, electricians, motor mechanics, and SHG products.
- **Report Updates**: Use the feedback box at the bottom of the portal to report any outdated numbers or suggest new local listings.
